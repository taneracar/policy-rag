# Equipment and Laptop Policy

## Standard laptop
New employees choose a 14-inch or 16-inch MacBook Pro, or a Lenovo ThinkPad for roles that need Windows. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## Monitors and accessories
Employees may order one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset from the IT catalog. Items outside the catalog are bought from the home office stipend.

## Lost or damaged equipment
Report a lost or stolen laptop to Security within 1 hour and to IT. The first accidental damage repair in a refresh cycle is covered by the company; repeated damage may need a manager review.

## Leaving the company
All company equipment is returned within 10 business days of the last working day, using the prepaid shipping box IT sends. Equipment that is not returned is deducted from the final paycheck where state law allows.
