# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Company accounts use single sign-on through Okta with a hardware security key or the Okta Verify app. Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Passwords are stored only in the company 1Password vault and are never shared, including with IT staff.

## Devices
Laptops must have full-disk encryption on and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within 14 days of release. Personal devices may access email and Slack only through the managed mobile app.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data and employee personal data are Restricted. Restricted data may not be copied to personal storage, removable media or unapproved AI tools.

## Approved AI tools
Only AI tools listed on the Security intranet page may be used with Internal or Confidential data. Restricted data may not be entered into any AI tool without a written exception from the Chief Information Security Officer.

## Reporting a security incident
Suspected incidents, including a lost or stolen device or a phishing email that was clicked, must be reported to security@ridgeview.example or in the #security-help Slack channel within 1 hour of discovery. Do not try to investigate or clean up on your own.
