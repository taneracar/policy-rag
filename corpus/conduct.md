# Code of Conduct: Gifts and Conflicts of Interest

## Gifts from vendors and customers
Employees may accept gifts or entertainment worth up to $100 per person per year from a single vendor or customer. Cash and gift cards are never accepted, whatever the amount. Gifts above the limit are returned or reported to Legal.

## Conflicts of interest
Outside jobs, board seats and investments in a competitor, vendor or customer must be disclosed to Legal before they start. Holding less than 1 percent of a public company's shares does not need disclosure.

## Speaking up
Concerns can be raised with a manager, People Operations, Legal, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. The company does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
