# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees in roles marked hybrid work from the office at least two days a week: Tuesday and Thursday are the team anchor days. Fully remote roles are approved by a vice president and recorded in Workday.

## Home office stipend
Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500, claimable during the first 90 days of employment. They also receive an internet allowance of $50 per month, paid through payroll.

## Working from another location
Employees may work from another U.S. state for up to 30 days per calendar year without approval. Working from another state for longer, or from any other country for any length of time, requires approval from People Operations and Legal at least 30 days in advance, because of tax and employment law.

## Core hours
Everyone is expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 Pacific Time on working days, whatever their location.

## Security when working remotely
Company work is done only on company-issued devices. Public Wi-Fi must be used with the company VPN turned on. See the Information Security Policy for details.
