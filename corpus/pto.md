# Paid Time Off Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) every pay period. Employees with less than three years of service accrue 15 days per year. Employees with three or more years of service accrue 20 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they expire. Unused PTO above the carryover limit is forfeited on January 1 and is not paid out, except where state law requires a payout.

## Requesting time off
Requests of three days or longer must be submitted in Workday at least two weeks in advance. Shorter requests need 48 hours of notice. Managers respond within three business days; a request with no response after three business days is treated as approved.

## Company holidays
The company observes 11 paid holidays each year, listed on the intranet holiday calendar. Holidays do not count against PTO. An employee required to work on a company holiday receives a floating day to use within 90 days.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Every employee receives 7 sick days per year, available on the first day of employment. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required only for absences of more than three consecutive working days.
