# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expenses are submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date. Every expense of $25 or more needs an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted after 60 days are reimbursed only with written approval from the Controller.

## Meals
While traveling overnight, the daily meal allowance is $75 per person, including tax and tip. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a vice president. Meals with clients are limited to $150 per attendee.

## Air travel
Book flights through Navan at least 14 days before departure when possible. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is allowed. Business class requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Lodging
Hotels are booked through Navan. The nightly hotel cap is $250 in most cities and $350 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle, London). Stays above the cap need manager approval before booking.

## Ground transportation and mileage
Rideshare and taxis are reimbursable for business travel. Personal car use is reimbursed at the IRS mileage rate in effect on the date of travel. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with a receipt. Traffic and parking fines are never reimbursed.

## Corporate card
Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Personal purchases on the corporate card are not allowed; an accidental personal charge must be repaid within 15 days.
