# Retirement and Learning Benefits

## 401(k) plan
Employees can join the 401(k) plan from their first day. The company matches 100 percent of employee contributions up to 4 percent of base salary. Company matching contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Certification exam fees count toward the budget; travel to a conference is paid from the team travel budget instead.

## Wellness
Employees receive $75 per month toward a gym membership, fitness classes or a wellness app, paid through payroll.

## Employee assistance program
Free, confidential counseling is available through the employee assistance program: up to 8 sessions per issue per year, also for household members.
