# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees with at least 6 months of service are eligible, regardless of gender, including adoptive and foster parents.

## Length of leave
The birthing parent receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents, adoptive and foster parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Returning to work
Employees may return on a reduced schedule of 60 percent time for the first 4 weeks after leave, at full pay. The employee's role, title and pay are protected while on leave.

## How to apply
Notify your manager and submit a leave request to People Operations at least 30 days before the expected start date, or as soon as practical.
