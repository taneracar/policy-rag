# Records and Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods

Loan files are kept for 7 years after the loan is paid off or closed. Customer account statements are kept for 5 years. Employee personnel files are kept for 4 years after separation. Email is kept for 3 years unless it is under legal hold.

## Legal hold

When the legal team issues a legal hold, the affected records may not be deleted or changed, even if their retention period has ended. Only the legal team can release a hold.

## Disposal

Paper records with customer information are shredded through the locked shred bins; recycling bins may not be used. Electronic media is wiped by IT before disposal.

## Customer deletion requests

Customer requests to delete personal data are routed to the privacy team, which answers within 45 days. Records the company is legally required to keep are excluded from deletion, and the customer is told which records were kept and why.
