# Travel and Expense Policy (2024 edition)

## Submitting expenses
Expenses are submitted in Concur within 45 days of the purchase date. Receipts are required for every expense of $50 or more.

## Meals
While traveling overnight, the daily meal allowance is $60 per person, including tax and tip. Client meals are limited to $120 per attendee.

## Lodging
The nightly hotel cap is $200 in all cities.

## Air travel
Economy class is required for all flights. Business class is not available.
